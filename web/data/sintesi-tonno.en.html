Tuna at a glance

Yellowfin tuna at the Rungis wholesale market closes week 39 at €13.20/kg, virtually unchanged on the week. Against the same period of 2025 the price is 2.1% lower. For late September it is a normal level. Over the last twelve months Italy imported 18,432 tonnes, 54% of it from Spain.
