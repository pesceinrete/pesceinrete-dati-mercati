Mussels at a glance

Mussels on Italian fish markets close week 39 at €2.06/kg, down 2.4% on the week. Against the same period of 2025 the price is 6.7% higher. For late September it is a high price: dearer than 96% of the comparable weeks of the last five years. Over the last twelve months Italy imported 35,464 tonnes, 55% of it from Spain.
