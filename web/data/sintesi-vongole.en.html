Clams at a glance

Clams on Italian fish markets close week 39 at €8.85/kg, slightly up 0.9% on the week and 5.4% on the month. Against the same period of 2025 the price is 4.2% higher. For late September it is a high price: dearer than 92% of the comparable weeks of the last five years. Over the last twelve months Italy imported 2,212 tonnes, 56% of it from Spain.
