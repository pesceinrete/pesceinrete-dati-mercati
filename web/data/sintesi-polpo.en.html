Octopus at a glance

Octopus at the Rungis wholesale market closes week 39 at €12.39/kg, up 2.3% on the week. For late September it is a high price: dearer than 84% of the comparable weeks of the last five years. Over the last twelve months Italy imported 23,175 tonnes, 56% of it from Morocco.
