Swordfish at a glance

Swordfish at the Rungis wholesale market closes week 39 at €14.02/kg, slightly down 1.9% on the week, though above a month ago by 2.1%. Against the same period of 2025 the price is 2.6% lower. For late September it is a high price: dearer than 88% of the comparable weeks of the last five years. Over the last twelve months Italy imported 11,070 tonnes, 55% of it from Spain.
