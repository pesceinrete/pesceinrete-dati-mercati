Swordfish at a glance

Swordfish imported into Italy closes week 46 at €12.47/kg, virtually unchanged on the week; on the month it is up 2.6%. Against the same period of 2025 the price is 7.7% higher. For mid-November it is a high price: dearer than 92% of the comparable weeks of the last three years. Over the last twelve months available, September 2025 to August 2026, Italy imported 20,592 tonnes, 66% of it from Spain, up 9% on the previous twelve.

Why fresh only?
Frozen swordfish arrives in irregular shipments of very different quality: its weekly average price is not readable. Fresh fillets and loins have regular arrivals and a stable price. Frozen is included in the monthly imports panel.
Is this the price I pay my supplier?
No. It is the average value declared at customs by importers into Italy, across all sizes. Use it to compare your own purchase price with the average and to read the trend, not as a quotation.
How up to date is it?
The data come out every week and run to about two weeks earlier: the most recent week is always incomplete at the source and is published only once it is closed.
