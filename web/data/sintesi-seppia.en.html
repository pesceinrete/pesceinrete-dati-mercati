Cuttlefish at a glance

Cuttlefish imported into Italy closes week 46 at €7.01/kg on a 4-week average, slightly down 0.6% on the week. Against the same period of 2025 the price is 11.0% lower. For mid-November it is a normal level. Volumes, 438 tonnes, are among the highest for the period. Over the last twelve months available, September 2025 to August 2026, Italy imported 12,298 tonnes, 22% of it from Senegal.

Why a four-week average?
The weight of each cuttlefish origin changes from week to week, and the single-week price jumps with the mix of shipments. The volume-weighted average of the last four weeks still updates every week and follows the real trend.
Is this the price I pay my supplier?
No. It is the average value declared at customs by importers into Italy, across all sizes. Use it to compare your own purchase price with the average and to read the trend, not as a quotation.
How up to date is it?
The data come out every week and run to about two weeks earlier: the most recent week is always incomplete at the source and is published only once it is closed.
