European sea bass at a glance

Sea bass at the Rungis wholesale market closes week 39 at €8.77/kg, virtually unchanged on the week; on the month it is up 5.5%. Against the same period of 2025 the price is 6.4% higher. For late September it is the highest level of the last five years. Over the last twelve months Italy imported 28,199 tonnes, 54% of it from Greece.

Why a French price?
There is no public weekly survey of sea bass prices in Italy. Rungis is the European wholesale market with the most continuous and documented survey.
