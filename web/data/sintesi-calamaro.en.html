Squid at a glance

Squid imported into Italy closes week 45 at €10.44/kg, down 5.5% on the week, though above a month ago by 3.7%. Against the same period of 2025 the price is 15.4% higher. For early November it is the highest level of the last three years. Over the last twelve months Italy imported 60,706 tonnes, 17% of it from Morocco, down 4% on the previous twelve.

What about flying squid?
It has its own customs codes and very different prices: this page covers only Loligo vulgaris squid, which customs identify separately. Keeping them apart is what makes the price readable.
Is this the price I pay my supplier?
No. It is the average value declared at customs by importers into Italy, across all sizes. Use it to compare your own purchase price with the average and to read the trend, not as a quotation.
How up to date is it?
The data come out every week and run to about two weeks earlier: the most recent week is always incomplete at the source and is published only once it is closed.
