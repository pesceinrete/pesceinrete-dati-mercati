Atlantic salmon at a glance

Salmon exported from Norway closes week 45 at €7.04/kg, down 2.6% on the week, though above a month ago by 4.3%. Against the same period of 2025 the price is 5.8% higher. For early November it is a high price: dearer than 84% of the comparable weeks of the last five years. Volumes, 28,277 tonnes, are among the highest for the period.
