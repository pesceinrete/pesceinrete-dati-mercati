Atlantic salmon at a glance

Salmon exported from Norway closes week 47 at €7.17/kg, up 3.3% on the week. For late November it is a normal level. Volumes, 25,761 tonnes, are among the highest for the period. Over the last twelve months available, September 2025 to August 2026, Italy imported 65,379 tonnes, 74% of it from Sweden, up 6% on the previous twelve.

Is this price high for the time of year?
The band at the top compares the week with the same weeks of the previous five years.
Is this the price my supplier is asking?
No. It is the average export value from Norway, across all sizes and destinations. It serves as a trend reference, not as a contractual quotation.
How much of it reaches Italy?
The flows panel shows monthly Italian imports and the rolling twelve-month total.
Why is Norway not among the origins?
Because Norwegian salmon enters the Union through other countries, mainly Nordic ones, and reaches Italy from there as intra-EU trade. Statistics record the country of dispatch, not the country of farming.
