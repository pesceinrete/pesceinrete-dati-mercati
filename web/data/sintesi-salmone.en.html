Atlantic salmon at a glance

Salmon exported from Norway closes week 39 at €6.68/kg, slightly down 1.8% on the week and 2.6% on the month. Against the same period of 2025 the price is 9.3% higher. For late September it is a high price: dearer than 84% of the comparable weeks of the last five years. Over the last twelve months Italy imported 25,291 tonnes, 53% of it from Norway, down 3% on the previous twelve.
