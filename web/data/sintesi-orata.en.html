Gilt-head sea bream at a glance

Sea bream imported into Italy from Türkiye closes week 46 at €5.31/kg, slightly up 1.0% on the week. Against the same period of 2025 the price is 13.1% lower. For mid-November it is a normal level. Volumes, 337 tonnes, are among the highest for the period. Over the last twelve months available, September 2025 to August 2026, Italy imported 40,437 tonnes, 41% of it from Greece, up 7% on the previous twelve.

Why the Turkish price?
Because it is the only one that exists every week: customs record imports from outside the Union, and Türkiye is the leading non-EU origin. Arrivals from Greece and Croatia are intra-EU trade and official statistics publish them only monthly: they are in the imports panel.
Is this the price I pay my supplier?
No. It is the average value declared at customs by importers into Italy, across all sizes. Use it to compare your own purchase price with the average and to read the trend, not as a quotation.
How up to date is it?
The data come out every week and run to about two weeks earlier: the most recent week is always incomplete at the source and is published only once it is closed.
