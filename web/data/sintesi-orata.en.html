Gilt-head sea bream at a glance

Sea bream at the Rungis wholesale market closes week 39 at €6.90/kg, slightly up 1.9% on the week and 6.3% on the month. Against the same period of 2025 the price is 4.5% higher. For late September it is the highest level of the last five years. Over the last twelve months Italy imported 33,074 tonnes, 55% of it from Greece, up 4% on the previous twelve.

Why a French price?
There is no public weekly survey of sea bream prices in Italy. Rungis is the European wholesale market with the most continuous and documented survey, and it tracks the same Greek and Turkish product that reaches Italy.
