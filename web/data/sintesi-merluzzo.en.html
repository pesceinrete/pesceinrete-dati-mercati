Cod at a glance

Cod at the Rungis wholesale market closes week 39 at €8.97/kg, down 2.1% on the week and 6.7% on the month. For late September it is a normal level. Over the last twelve months Italy imported 46,413 tonnes, 53% of it from Norway, up 9% on the previous twelve.
