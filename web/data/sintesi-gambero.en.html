Shrimp at a glance

Shrimp imported into Italy from Ecuador closes week 45 at €5.53/kg, up 4.1% on the week and 12.0% on the month. Against the same period of 2025 the price is 5.2% lower. For early November it is a low price: cheaper than 85% of the comparable weeks of the last three years. Volumes, 1,102 tonnes, are among the highest for the period. Over the last twelve months Italy imported 85,818 tonnes, 50% of it from Ecuador.

Why does only Ecuador have a weekly price?
Because Ecuador ships a homogeneous product, vannamei, and the average price moves with the market. Argentina and India ship different products and sizes: from one week to the next the average price jumps with the mix of shipments, so we show it as the average of the last four weeks.
Is this the price I pay my supplier?
No. It is the average value declared at customs by importers into Italy, across all sizes. Use it to compare your own purchase price with the average and to read the trend, not as a quotation.
How up to date is it?
The data come out every week and run to about two weeks earlier: the most recent week is always incomplete at the source and is published only once it is closed.
