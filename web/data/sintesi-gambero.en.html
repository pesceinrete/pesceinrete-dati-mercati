Shrimp at a glance

Shrimp imported into Italy from Ecuador closes week 46 at €5.03/kg, down 9.2% on the week, though above a month ago by 1.2%. Against the same period of 2025 the price is 14.7% lower. For mid-November it is the lowest level of the last three years. Over the last twelve months available, September 2025 to August 2026, Italy imported 85,818 tonnes, 50% of it from Ecuador.

Why does only Ecuador have a weekly price?
Because Ecuador ships a homogeneous product, shrimps of the genus Penaeus almost all under the same customs code, and the average price moves with the market. Argentina and India ship different products and sizes: from one week to the next the average price jumps with the mix of shipments, so we show it as the average of the last four weeks.
Is this the price I pay my supplier?
No. It is the average value declared at customs by importers into Italy, across all sizes. Use it to compare your own purchase price with the average and to read the trend, not as a quotation.
How up to date is it?
The data come out every week and run to about two weeks earlier: the most recent week is always incomplete at the source and is published only once it is closed.
