Shrimp at a glance

Shrimp at the Rungis wholesale market closes week 39 at €10.70/kg, virtually unchanged on the week; on the month it is up 9.9%. Against the same period of 2025 the price is 13.2% higher. For late September it is the highest level of the last five years. Over the last twelve months Italy imported 51,369 tonnes, 55% of it from Ecuador.
