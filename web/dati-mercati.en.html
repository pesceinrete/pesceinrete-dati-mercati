Price history

The price is converted to euro at the average exchange rate of the same week, not at today's rate.

Compare periods

The period price is the volume-weighted average of weekly prices. Comparisons between periods of different length are flagged and totals are not expressed as percentages.

From the reference to your cost

The dashed rule signals that this is not a source: it is a simulation built on the parameters you enter. It brings the market reference down to the product as you buy and process it.

How these parameters are used

Mark-up and freight are added to the reference and give the landed cost. Glaze is water: on a frozen product with 10% glaze the net product costs the landed cost divided by 0.90. Yield is what remains after processing, and divides the cost again.

The result is for comparing different offers on the same basis, not for setting a purchase price. None of the parameters is stored or sent anywhere.

    How we read seasonality

    The band collects the values of the same weeks in the previous five years, taken in a window of two weeks before and two after. The percentile says how many of those values sit below this week's price.

    Comparing with last year alone is not enough: a single year may have been anomalous. With fewer than three years of history or fewer than twelve usable observations the seasonal reading is not published.
